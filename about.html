About

Ten years of margin notes.

"This is where
your voice really
comes through."

I've spent the last ten years teaching high school English, including AP Literature and Composition and AP Language and Composition. Most of that time has been spent doing exactly what tutoring asks of me: reading a student's writing closely, figuring out what they're actually trying to say, and helping them say it better.

I tutor two kinds of writing. The first is the college application essay — personal statements and supplements, where the goal is a piece of writing that sounds like the student and not like anyone else's advice. The second is exam writing — the timed, structured essays the AP Lit and AP Lang exams ask for, where strategy and practice matter as much as raw skill. I also work with students on general high school English: essays, literary analysis, grammar, and reading comprehension, at whatever level they're starting from.

My approach is direct but not harsh. I'll tell you what's working, what isn't, and exactly what to do about it — the same feedback I'd want if I were the one writing.
